The Story

Coco and Eliot are best friends who are always together. One day, something important goes missing and they decide they are going to find it.

They have completely different personalities, but they make a great team.

The Characters

Coco

Coco has bleach blonde hair and huge glasses that take up almost half of her face. She is curious, dramatic, and always ready to do something fun.

Eliot

Eliot has dirty blonde hair, thick eyebrows, long eyelashes, and a soft button nose. She is more careful than Coco and usually thinks before making a decision.

Coco and Eliot are best friends.

The Adventure

Coco and Eliot decide to retrace their steps and look for clues that will help them find what went missing.

What They Bring

  • Coco's glasses
  • Eliot's notebook
  • Their phones
  • Snacks

The Plan

  1. Go back to where they started
  2. Look for clues
  3. Ask people for help
  4. Follow the clues
  5. Find the missing item

Their number one rule is to stick together.
